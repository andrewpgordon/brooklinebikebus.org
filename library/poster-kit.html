Putting it together

  1. Print pages 2 to 8 at actual size (choose "Actual size" or 100%, not "Fit to page"). Color is best, but black and white still works.
  2. Cut each piece on its dashed line. Cut around the dragon badge.
  3. Lay everything out before you glue. Butt BIKE and BUS right up against each other so they read as one line across the top.
  4. Glue stick or spray adhesive both work. A foam board is sturdier to hold for 20 minutes than a paper poster board.

Standing out front

  • People driving past can read BIKE BUS and the date. The QR code is only for parents who walk up, so it sits low on the board.
  • Test the QR code with your phone before you go.
  • The sidewalk is public. If you want to stand on school grounds, check with the office first.

Keep it to red and black on white. Gold letters are hard to read from a distance.
