What's a
bike bus?

A group of kids and parents riding to school together, on a set route at a set time, going at the pace of the slowest kid. Grown-ups ride at the front and the back.

Three quarters of a mile from Griggs Park to the Driscoll racks, nothing steeper than 3%, and we never ride on Beacon Street. We cross it on foot.

Not sure your
kid is ready?

Plenty of kids in the group will be new at this. Ride right next to them, put a younger one on a bike seat or a tag-along, or walk any part of it you want to walk.

Scooters, balance bikes and cargo bikes are all welcome. You're also welcome to come and watch this time and ride with us in November.

Can you help
lead it?

We could use a few more grown-ups on Wednesday morning. Ride at the back with the pump. Stand on a corner in a vest so cars wait while the kids go past. Take some photos.

None of it needs any experience, and you'll be riding with your own kid either way.
