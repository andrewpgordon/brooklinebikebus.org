The Driscoll
Bike Bus

Kids and parents biking to school together. We go at the speed of the slowest kid, with adults at the front and the back.

Route map, questions and the sign-up form. One form per family.

Every child rides with their own parent or guardian. Please don't drop kids off.

Helmets are required for everyone, adults too.

We ride with traffic, on the right side of the road, and we never ride on Beacon Street.

Still learning to ride? Ride right next to your child or walk alongside. That's completely fine.

If it rains, we'll send a message by 6:15 AM.

We need a few more adult helpers. You can offer on the sign-up form.
