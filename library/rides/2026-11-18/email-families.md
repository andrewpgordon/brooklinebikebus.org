# Email to the families, Nov 18 ride

Goes to `driscoll-bike-bus@googlegroups.com`. These are people who already rode
or already signed up, so it assumes they know what a bike bus is and spends its
first paragraph on what changed instead.

**Subject:** Bike bus number two, Wednesday November 18

---

Hi everyone,

The second Driscoll Bike Bus is Wednesday, November 18. Two things have changed
since October, so please read this bit even if you came last time.

There's no walking group down Corey Hill any more, and Washington Square isn't a
pickup. Everybody meets at the northeast corner of Griggs Park between 7:15 and
7:30, however you get there. That's the only place we collect people.

How the morning goes:

7:15 to 7:30. Gather at the northeast corner of Griggs Park.

7:30. We leave, along Griggs Terrace and Griggs Road. We stop at Washington
Street and wait for everyone to catch up.

7:38. Washington Square. We bunch up again, then cross Beacon Street all
together on one light.

7:45. Bike racks at school. The bell is at 8:00.

Bring a helmet, which everyone wears, grown-ups included. A bike or a scooter.
Gloves and something over your ears, because it's November and 7:15 is colder
than it looks out of the window.

The only thing your child needs to be able to do is ride or scoot at walking
speed or faster. Fifteen kids came in October and a lot of them were new at it.

Four jobs we could use help with, and you can just reply to this email:

Sweep, riding at the back behind the last child with a pump and a patch kit.
This one is open and it's the one we most need.

Corkers, riding ahead to hold the side streets while the group goes past. No
experience needed. It has to be an adult riding without a child of their own,
since every kid stays with their own grown-up.

Photos.

Donuts, which is the job with the best return on effort.

Please sign up at brooklinebikebus.org/driscoll even if you rode in October, one
form per family. It's how we know how many donuts to buy.

The October album is still up and November's pictures will go in a new one. If
you're not in the album yet and you'd like to be, tell me. If there's a picture
of your child in there you'd rather wasn't, tell me that instead and it comes
out.

If we have to cancel for weather we'll send word here by 6:15 that morning.

Thanks,
