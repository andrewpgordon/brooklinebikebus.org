# Email to the Driscoll bulletin, Nov 18 ride

A cover note plus the blurb to run. Nicole has the contact at the school from
the September slot.

This one is longer than the family email and the WhatsApp message on purpose:
it goes to people who have never heard of the bike bus, so it has to say what
one is. Two lines can't be cut whatever the space, the one saying this is
organized by parents and isn't a school program, and the one saying every child
rides with their own parent or guardian. Both are from
`docs/release-language.md`.

No mention of photos. The sign-up form still has no photo question, and a school
bulletin is the wrong place to raise it first.

If they want a picture, use `library/assets/driscoll-badge-print.png` or the
flyer. Not the school's logo.

## The blurb (about 110 words)

**Bike to school together on Wednesday, November 18**

Driscoll parents are running a bike bus on Wednesday, November 18. A bike bus is
a group of kids and parents riding to school together on a set route, at the
speed of the slowest rider. Fifteen kids came to the first one in October.

Everyone meets at the northeast corner of Griggs Park between 7:15 and 7:30, and
we're at the bike racks by 7:45, fifteen minutes before the bell. Every child
rides with their own parent or guardian, and everyone wears a helmet, grown-ups
too.

Please sign up at brooklinebikebus.org/driscoll, where the route and the full
times are.

The bike bus is organized by parents. It isn't a program of the Driscoll School,
the Public Schools of Brookline or the Driscoll PTO. Questions to Andrew Gordon,
andrewpgordon@gmail.com.
