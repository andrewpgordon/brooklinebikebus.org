## Full version (about 200 words)

**Bike to school together on Wednesday, November 18**

Driscoll parents are running a bike bus on Wednesday, November 18. A bike bus is
a group of kids and parents riding to school together on a set route, at the
speed of the slowest rider, with adults at the front and the back. The first one
in October brought fifteen kids to school on bikes, and anyone can come to this
one.

Everyone meets at the northeast corner of Griggs Park between 7:15 and 7:30. We
leave at 7:30, ride along Griggs Terrace and Griggs Road, cross Beacon Street at
Washington Square all together on one light, and get to the bike racks at 7:45,
fifteen minutes before the bell. Griggs Park is the only meeting place.

Every child rides with their own parent or guardian, and everyone wears a
helmet, grown-ups too. Your child needs to be able to ride or scoot at walking
pace or faster, and that's the only requirement. Scooters, balance bikes,
tag-alongs and bike seats are all welcome. Bring gloves, because it's cold at
7:15 in November.

Sign up at brooklinebikebus.org/driscoll, one form per family. We're also
looking for a few adults to ride at the back and at the side streets.

The bike bus is organized by parents. It isn't a program of the Driscoll School,
the Public Schools of Brookline or the Driscoll PTO. Questions to Andrew Gordon
at andrewpgordon@gmail.com or Nicole McClelland at nicole.mcclelland@gmail.com.
