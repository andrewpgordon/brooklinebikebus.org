What to know

  • Wear a helmet. Kids and grown-ups.
  • Come with your child. These are parent-organized rides, so every child rides with their own grown-up.
  • Check which route it is before you leave the house. The two start in different places.
  • Check the bike the night before. Air in the tires and brakes that work.
  • Younger siblings and grandparents are welcome to ride along.
  • Starting one at your school? Massachusetts Safe Routes to School will help, at no cost.

Questions about the Lincoln ride?
