What to know

  • Wear a helmet. Kids and grown-ups.
  • Come with your child. Family members are welcome to ride along.
  • Check your bike the night before. Air in the tires and brakes that work.
  • Not a Pierce family? The Driscoll bike bus rides on Wednesday, October 7.
  • Starting one at your school? Massachusetts Safe Routes to School will help, at no cost.

We don't organize this ride. The Pierce bike bus is run by volunteers from the Brookline Safe Routes to School Task Force. We list it here because this site is for bike buses across Brookline, not only Driscoll.

These details come from the Pierce flyer that went around in September 2026. If something has changed since, the Pierce school community will have the current version. Each child rides in the care of their own parent or guardian.

Riding at Driscoll instead?

The Driscoll bike bus is on Wednesday, October 7. It gathers at Griggs Park at 7:15 and gets to school by 7:45.
