Hello,

I'm a Driscoll School parent, and Nicole McClelland and I are organizing a
bike bus for the morning of Wednesday, November 18. A bike bus is a group of
kids and parents riding to school together. We ran the first one on October 7,
two of your Bicycle Squad units came out for it, and I wanted to say thank you
for that and ask about the next one.

We're expecting around 15 to 25 kids, each with their own parent.

- 7:15 to 7:30: everyone gathers at the northeast corner of Griggs Park. That's
  the only meeting place.
- 7:30: we ride along Griggs Terrace and Griggs Road to Washington Street.
- About 7:38: the whole group crosses Beacon Street at Washington Square
  together, riding, on one light. The town crossing guards held the traffic for
  us in October and it worked well.
- 7:45: we arrive at the Driscoll bike racks.

We never ride along Beacon Street. We cross it once, as one group, and nobody
rides on from the far side until everyone is over. Adults ride at the front and
the back.

Our questions:

1. Is there anything about the route or the timing that worries you?
2. Would it be possible to have officers at Washington Square again around 7:35
   to 7:45 on November 18? That's the one place the group crosses Beacon Street.
3. Is the crossing guard at Washington Square posted there every school morning
   at that time, or was October 7 a one-off? It makes a difference to how we
   plan the crossing.
4. Is there anyone else in town we should let know?

Tina Hein from Massachusetts Safe Routes to School is copied here. She has
been advising us and is happy to answer questions about how bike buses usually
work.
